## Headline (my reading; inferences labelled)

1. **No 2570–2573 schedule is published yet.** None was found on the Treasury central notice list, on the notice lists of Nong Khai and Nakhon Phanom, or in the October 2026 provincial news. For Chiang Rai and Phayao, see §3e of the method notes.
2. **Until about 20 October 2026, provinces were preparing the round for a 1 January 2027 start.**
   - Nong Khai's committee met on 19 Aug 2026 and Trang's on 20 Oct 2026. Both considered the 2570–2573 schedules "สำหรับประกาศใช้วันที่ 1 มกราคม 2570" (*for use from 1 January 2027*).
3. **From 28 October 2026, Treasury sources talk about an *extension* of the 2566–2569 schedules.**
   - On 28 Oct 2026 the Suphanburi office joined a video-conference "เพื่อซักซ้อมความเข้าใจการขยายประกาศใช้บัญชีราคาประเมินทรัพย์สิน" (*to brief staff on extending the use of the appraisal schedules*).
   - On 2 Nov 2026 the Chachoengsao committee had two agenda items: "การขยายเวลาให้ใช้บัญชีราคาประเมินที่ดิน … รอบบัญชี พ.ศ. 2566–2569" (*extending the time for using the land … appraisal schedules of the 2023–2026 round*) and setting a new land-survey area and period.
   - This matches the one-year extension in Act s. 19 para. 3, which needs central-committee approval.
   - **Update 3 Nov 2026:** see the recheck above. Chainat states one year (1 Jan to 31 Dec 2027), and Chainat and Phatthalung name the deed schedule. Still not established whether the extension is nationwide or approved.
   - **Not established (as of 2 Nov):**
     - whether the extension is nationwide or covers only some provinces;
     - whether it covers the per-parcel deed schedule (the RQ1 data) or only the other-title-type land and building schedules;
     - its length.
   - No central-committee notice, Royal Gazette notice, Cabinet item or press report confirming it was found.
4. **The market-data survey window for the 2027 round is not stated in any source found.**
   - The one survey notice I could read (Suphanburi, signed 10 Nov 2025) sets a land survey from **24 Nov 2025 to 30 Sep 2026**. It does not say which round it serves, and a survey period is not the same thing as the date range of the transactions used.
5. **Precedent: both earlier delays were decided late, about 6–15 weeks before the planned start date, and by administrative act, not by law.**
   - 15 Oct 2019: the Treasury announced that the 1 Jan 2020 start would move to 1 Jan 2021.
   - About 6 Jan 2021: the Permanent Secretary of Finance signed a further one-year delay.
   - 18 Nov 2021: the central committee signed the notice setting 1 Jan 2023.
   - All dates come from press reports (secondary). The primary instruments were not opened.

## Implications for the pre-analysis plan timing

The safe deadline is still **2 Dec 2026**. If the extension in §B turns out to be national and to include the deed schedule, publication moves to late 2027 (start 1 Jan 2028), and there is more time. But provincial committees have already been looking at 2027 values (Nong Khai, Trang), and the Nong Khai precedent shows publication can come **13 months** before the start date. So "before publication" cannot be proven from the start date alone.

- **Register now** (before any provincial 2570–2573 notice appears), not at the last moment.
- Make the plan **robust to a one-year shift**: define the post-period as "the first schedule under s. 19 issued after the 2566–2569 schedule, whatever its start date".
- Add a **pre-specified rule for survey-window timing**: the 2024 flood counts as pre-survey only if a provincial survey notice or committee record shows a data window that starts after October 2024; otherwise, report it as ambiguous.
- **User actions:**
  - check Ratchakitcha in a browser for an extension notice;
  - ask the Chachoengsao, Nong Khai, Chiang Rai and Phayao offices whether the 2566–2569 deed schedule is being extended, and to what date.
