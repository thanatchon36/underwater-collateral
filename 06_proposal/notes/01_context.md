## Reasoning

The section opens with the supervisory template, because it is the frame a reader will recognise. It then shows that every step in the template is an assumption tested mostly where insurance exists. From there it argues that Thailand is a laboratory, and the three reasons are mechanisms, not local colour:
- insurance is switched off;
- relief follows rules;
- parcel-level official values are public.
