# Data request drafts (for the author to rewrite and send)

**Working draft, 2026-11-02.** Two requests for public information that the RQ1 pre-analysis plan uses. They are written for the author to send personally and ask only for information the agencies already publish or announce. Replace every bracketed item before sending. Nothing is sent from this workspace.

Why each request matters:
- **Request 1 (DDPM provincial offices):** the PAP's administrative-spillover sensitivity drops control sheets in districts with a 2024 emergency-zone declaration (PAP §3). A time-boxed search of the provincial open-data catalogues on 2026-11-02 found no 2024 list for any of the four provinces (`data_access_plan.md`). The drop is coded (`rq1_declarations.py`, schema `03_data/schemas/ddpm_declarations_2024.md`) and runs as soon as a list is entered; without one the sensitivity is not reported, and the registration says so.
- **Request 2 (Treasury provincial offices):** whether the 2027–30 round was surveyed before or after the 2024 floods decides the timing case (PAP §9). If the survey period is unknown when the round is published, case (a) is assumed and case (b) reported as a sensitivity.
