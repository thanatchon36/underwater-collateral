## A. Who designates "Level 4", at what geographic unit, and is there a list?

### A1. Correction to the premise: the 2 Dec 2025 Cabinet resolution did not define Level 4

There are three separate instruments, and each has its own geography.

| Instrument | Who | Area | Applies to | Source (locator) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| คำสั่งนายกรัฐมนตรี ที่ 10/2568 เรื่อง ยกระดับการจัดการสาธารณภัยร้ายแรงอย่างยิ่ง (ระดับ 4) ในพื้นที่ในจังหวัดสงขลา (Prime Minister's Order 10/2568: raising disaster management to Level 4, extremely severe, in areas of Songkhla Province) | Prime Minister, under s. 31 of the Disaster Prevention and Mitigation Act B.E. 2550 and the National Disaster Prevention and Mitigation Plan 2564–2570 | Songkhla Province | Defines the Level-4 zone that BOT circular ว.8158/2568 uses for commercial banks and their group subsidiaries | Named in BOT ว.8158/2568, p. 1 (scanned page), para. 2. The order itself was not opened (see A3). |
| มติคณะรัฐมนตรี 2 ธันวาคม 2568 มาตรการด้านการเงินเพื่อช่วยเหลือ เยียวยาและฟื้นฟูผู้ประสบอุทกภัยภาคใต้ (Cabinet resolution of 2 Dec 2025: financial measures to assist, compensate and restore southern flood victims) | Cabinet, on a Ministry of Finance proposal | Southern flood victims "according to DDPM announcements" (ตามประกาศกรมป้องกันและบรรเทาสาธารณภัย (ปภ.)). The press release lists 12 affected provinces. | Specialised financial institutions (SFIs) only. Principal and interest moratorium for 12 months at 0% interest, up to THB 1,000,000 per borrower per SFI, applications by 31 Jan 2026. Plus two 0% loan schemes. Commercial banks and non-banks were left to MoF and BOT to design. | MoF Fiscal Policy Office press release ฉบับที่ 164/2568 (2 Dec 2025), p. 1 (items 1–3) and p. 2 (commercial banks) |
| BOT ว.8158/2568 (15 Dec 2025) | BOT, after a MoF letter and FIPC approval | "เขตพื้นที่เกิดสาธารณภัยร้ายแรงอย่างยิ่ง (ระดับ 4)" (Level-4 zone) | Banks and their group subsidiaries: retail and SME debtors not NPL on 2 Dec 2025. SFIs instead follow "มาตรการและพื้นที่ที่กำหนดในมติคณะรัฐมนตรี เมื่อวันที่ 2 ธันวาคม 2568" (the measures and areas set in the Cabinet resolution). | ว.8158 p. 2 item 2; Q&A item 11 (p. 10) |

Two consequences follow:
- The Cabinet resolution's only role in the BOT circular is to fix the **date**: the NPL cut-off and the start of the 12-month relaxation windows (ว.8158 p. 1, where "วันที่คณะรัฐมนตรีมีมติฯ" is defined as 2 Dec 2025; items 2.1–2.5).
- The Level-4 **area** comes from the Prime Minister's order. Commercial-bank and SFI relief therefore cover **different geographies**: Level 4 (Songkhla) for commercial banks, and DDPM-announced areas in up to 12 provinces for SFIs.

### A2. Unit of designation and who designates

- **Legal basis (verified).** Section 31 of the Disaster Prevention and Mitigation Act B.E. 2550 (Royal Gazette vol. 124, part 52 ก, 7 Sep 2007; p. 14 of the DDPM-hosted copy) reads: "ในกรณีที่เกิดสาธารณภัยร้ายแรงอย่างยิ่ง นายกรัฐมนตรีหรือรองนายกรัฐมนตรีซึ่งนายกรัฐมนตรีมอบหมาย มีอำนาจสั่งการ … ให้ความช่วยเหลือแก่ประชาชนในพื้นที่ที่กำหนดก็ได้" ("in an extremely severe disaster, the Prime Minister, or a Deputy PM so assigned, may direct … assistance to people **in a designated area**").
  - So **the Prime Minister designates**, not DDPM, provincial governors or the Cabinet.
  - The Act leaves the area unit to the order itself.
  - The four-level scale comes from the National Disaster Prevention and Mitigation Plan 2564–2570, as BOT ว.8158 p. 1 cites it. I did not open that plan [UNVERIFIED for the definitions of levels 1–4].
- **Unit actually used in 2025: the whole province of Songkhla**, with two caveats.
  - BOT's description is "ในพื้นที่ในจังหวัดสงขลา" ("in areas of Songkhla Province") (ว.8158 p. 1).
  - The joint BOT and Thai Bankers' Association release of 15 Dec 2025 (p. 1) asks "ลูกหนี้ … ในเขตพื้นที่สาธารณภัยร้ายแรงอย่างยิ่ง (ระดับ 4) ในจังหวัดสงขลา" to verify identity and damage with their bank **by 31 Jan 2026**. It names no other province and no district.
  - The operative text of Order 10/2568, as reproduced by the press, is "กำหนดให้พื้นที่จังหวัดสงขลาเป็นพื้นที่เกิดสาธารณภัยร้ายแรงอย่างยิ่ง (ระดับ 4)". It was signed and took effect on 25 Nov 2025 (Thansettakij, 25 Nov 2025, <https://www.thansettakij.com/economy/644931>). **[UNVERIFIED: press reproduction; original order not opened]**
  - Caveat 1: the circular introduces the order with "เช่น" ("for example"), so other Level-4 orders cannot be ruled out. No BOT, MoF or AHA document I read names any Level-4 area other than Songkhla.
- **Duration.** The press reports that the Prime Minister downgraded Songkhla to Level 3 on 5 Dec 2025 and that the forward command was closed on 14 Dec 2025 (Prachachat, 14 Dec 2025, <https://www.prachachat.net/politics/news-1936645>) [UNVERIFIED]. BOT relief does not depend on how long Level 4 lasted: relief is anchored to 2 Dec 2025 and to the borrower's opt-in date.
- **Lower-tier declarations (the ว.601 template).** ว.8158 Q&A item 5 (p. 7) lets a lender backdate the moratorium to the date when "ผู้มีอำนาจออกประกาศเขตพื้นที่ประสบสาธารณภัยตามกฎหมายว่าด้วยการป้องกันและบรรเทาสาธารณภัย" declared the disaster zone, or when a command notified a yellow, orange or red situation level. Two things here were not verified:
  - that the competent authority is the provincial governor, acting as provincial director, and that zones are declared at district, subdistrict or village level [UNVERIFIED]. The pattern appears in press reports of DDPM declarations, which count อำเภอ / ตำบล / หมู่บ้าน. I did not open the governing Ministry of Finance or Ministry of Interior regulation.
  - The Act's s. 21 assigns first response to the local director and notification of the district and provincial directors; it does not itself describe the zone declaration.

### A3. Are designation lists or maps published?

- **Level 4:** no list or map is attached to BOT ว.8158, the 15 Dec release, the FIPC report or the MoF release. The only public statement of the area is "Songkhla Province".
  - The Royal Gazette (ratchakitcha.soc.go.th) returned HTTP 403 (Cloudflare), as in the earlier pass, so I could not check whether Order 10/2568 was gazetted.
  - thaigov.go.th news pages now load their content through JavaScript, and curl returned only the page shell.
  - A search snippet attributes this to a thaigov.go.th item of 25 Nov 2025: "ผู้ว่าราชการจังหวัดที่ประสบภัยประกาศเป็นพื้นที่ประสบภัยพิบัติแล้ว" ("governors of affected provinces have already declared disaster areas") [UNVERIFIED: snippet only].
- **DDPM area declarations:**
  - DDPM provincial mini-sites host declaration notices (e.g. `ska.disaster.go.th` for Songkhla; a Chiang Mai notice at <https://cmi.disaster.go.th/CMI/about?id=32306>), but the content comes from an API that needs a session token (HTTP 401, "Token not found"). I did not try to get round it.
  - No DDPM declaration dataset was found on data.go.th or catalog.disaster.go.th in one search.
  - **Next step:** open `ska.disaster.go.th` and the other southern provincial DDPM sites in a browser, or ask DDPM (ศูนย์ข้อมูลสาธารณภัย, datacenter.disaster.go.th) for the 2025 declarations by village, subdistrict and date.
- **Usable for intention to treat?**
  - Commercial-bank moratorium: yes, but only at **province level**. Treatment is a borrower whose collateral or address is in Songkhla, not NPL on 2 Dec 2025, and under the per-group limits.
  - SFI relief and ว.601 template relief: not yet, because they need the DDPM village or subdistrict lists, which are not in hand.

## B. Geography and scale of the late-2025 floods

### B1. Southern floods, mid-November to December 2025

- **Onset and provinces.**
  - MoF release 164/2568 (p. 1): floods "ตั้งแต่ช่วงกลางเดือนพฤศจิกายน 2568" (from mid-November 2025) spread to 12 provinces: Prachuap Khiri Khan, Chumphon, Surat Thani, Krabi, Nakhon Si Thammarat, Trang, Phatthalung, Satun, Songkhla, Pattani, Narathiwat and Yala.
  - AHA Centre Flash Update No. 1 (25 Nov 2025), using DDPM data: flooding from 17 Nov 2025 in the same 12 provinces. As of 25 Nov, 06:00:
    - cumulative: 112 districts, 696 subdistricts, 5,063 villages, 806,677 households (2,186,444 people);
    - still flooded: 9 provinces (Surat Thani, Nakhon Si Thammarat, Trang, Phatthalung, Satun, Songkhla, Pattani, Yala, Narathiwat), 98 districts, 643 subdistricts, 4,688 villages, 798,695 households (2,196,758 people);
    - 13 deaths: Nakhon Si Thammarat 6, Pattani 3, Yala 2, Phatthalung 2.
- **Peak scale.** AHA Weekly Disaster Update, week 48 (24–30 Nov 2025), citing DDPM, as of 1 Dec 18:00:
  - 1.46 million families (3.99 million people) in the 12 provinces initially affected;
  - 9 provinces still inundated, with 909,000 families (2.3 million people) affected;
  - **177 deaths**.

  The ADINet record AHA-FL-2025-001484-THA (closed, last updated 17 Dec 2025) shows 276 dead, 4,110,673 affected persons and 1,501,148 families in its impact panel. Its narrative contradicts the panel on deaths, so the record is not reliable for death counts.
- **Songkhla and Hat Yai.** BOT Monetary Policy Report Q4 2025, p. 14 ("สถานการณ์อุทกภัยภาคใต้"):
  - Hat Yai timeline: rain and first ponding 19–20 Nov; high water and economic standstill 21–27 Nov; water receding 28–29 Nov; recovery from 30 Nov.
  - About 1 million households and more than 10,000 businesses affected, especially trade and tourism SMEs.
  - GDP impact −0.1 to −0.2%, mostly in Songkhla.
  - High-impact province (1.4% of national GDP): **Songkhla only**. Moderate-impact (4.0% of GDP): Nakhon Si Thammarat, Pattani, Phatthalung, Narathiwat, Yala, Satun, Trang and Surat Thani.
  - About 90% of factories were not flooded.
- **Satellite activations.**
  - International Charter Space and Major Disasters, Activation 1004:
    - activated 25 Nov 2025, requested by UNOSAT on behalf of UN ESCAP, project management by UNITAR;
    - the page summarises rain on 19–23 Nov affecting 16 districts and 100 subdistricts, 635 mm in three days at Hat Yai, and a state of emergency in Songkhla;
    - products include "Satellite-detected water extents over southern provinces of Thailand" (26 Nov), "Satellite-detected water extents over Hat Yai District, Songkhla Province" (26 Nov) and a Chana impact map (6 Dec).
  - Sentinel Asia (requested by GISTDA, activated 25 Nov 2025):
    - Sentinel-1 flood products of 23 Nov for Songkhla and Phatthalung (AIT VAP001), Pattani, Yala and Narathiwat (VAP002) and Hat Yai (VAP003);
    - ALOS-2 products of 22 Nov and 1 Dec.
  - No Copernicus EMS rapid-mapping activation for Thailand in 2025 turned up in one search [not found, not proven absent].
- **BOT releases.**
  - 24 Nov 2025 (news-20251124): BOT urged lenders to apply the ว.601/2567 template in the southern region: card minimum payments, emergency personal-loan limits and restructuring within 12 months of a zone's declaration, with classification kept at its pre-disaster level. The page names no province. Its PDF version returned 404, so the page was read through the fetch tool.
  - 15 Dec 2025 (news-20251215-2, PDF read):
    - the joint BOT and Thai Bankers' Association package;
    - per-group limits: SME and housing (including home-for-cash) THB 3 m; car hire-purchase or title loans THB 800,000; motorcycles THB 50,000; cards and personal loans THB 100,000;
    - debtors outside Level 4 receive each bank's existing measures (p. 2).
- **FIPC report, H2 2025** (pp. 1, 3; PDF dated 5 Mar 2026):
  - the Financial Institutions Policy Committee approved the moratorium for SME and retail debtors in Level-4 zones after a **Ministry of Finance letter** asked BOT to act;
  - it is **funded by a temporary cut in the FIDF fee for the 2026 remittance round**;
  - it approved the LTV and risk-weight relief for repair loans, and NPA relief "ที่ตั้งอยู่ในพื้นที่ที่เกิดสาธารณภัยร้ายแรงอย่างยิ่ง (ระดับ 4)" (located in Level-4 areas);
  - all effective from 15 Dec 2025.
- **ว.8158 details not yet recorded in `policy_timeline.md`** (both on the scanned p. 4):
  - item 2.5: current-EIR income recognition on modified loans;
  - item 2.6: days past due may be kept at their pre-disaster count for assisted debtors not yet impaired; footnote 8 extends this to debtors who were affected or could not travel because of the disaster.

### B2. Do public sources match the project's 2025 radar geography?

Partly. The nine radar provinces fall into **two separate 2025 events**, and only one of them is the Level-4 event.

| Radar province (sheets) | Public source | Event | Level 4? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Songkhla (113) | All sources above; the only high-impact province in the BOT MPR | Southern floods, 19–30 Nov 2025 | **Yes** |
| Pattani (40), Phatthalung (27), Nakhon Si Thammarat (26) | MoF 164/2568; AHA FU1; BOT MPR (moderate impact); Sentinel Asia products for Phatthalung and Pattani | Southern floods, Nov 2025 | No (DDPM-declared areas → SFI and ว.601 relief only) |

| Nakhon Sawan (33) | AHA Weekly Disaster Update 8–14 Sep 2025: DDPM "documented flooding across Phitsanulok, Phetchabun, Phichit, Nakhon Sawan, Uthai Thani, Chai Nat, Sing Buri…" | Same central event, Sep 2025 | No |

- The northern provinces fall inside the radar's northern window (15 Aug–31 Oct, `05_design/pre_analysis_plan_rq1_draft.md` l. 20) and match the Aug–Sep events. The southern provinces fall inside the 15 Nov–31 Dec window.
- Songkhla's dominance matches the public record.
- Five southern provinces that public sources list as still flooded on 25 Nov (Narathiwat, Yala, Surat Thani, Trang, Satun) are not among the radar's ≥25-sheet provinces. This fits the documented radar weakness for short or flash flooding and water among buildings (*interpretation*).
- The public sources give provinces and counts, not extents. Only the Charter and Sentinel Asia products give footprints that can be compared with the radar sheets.

## Implications for the RQ2 2025 design

The 2025 "Level-4 boundary" in the proposal and in `policy_timeline.md` (identification item 2) is, on present evidence, the **provincial border of Songkhla**. It is not a fine-grained line drawn by the Cabinet. The commercial-bank moratorium is therefore an intention-to-treat contrast at province level. Its comparison group is flooded borrowers in neighbouring Phatthalung, Pattani and Nakhon Si Thammarat, who were not untreated: they were eligible for template relief under ว.601/2567 and, at SFIs, a Cabinet moratorium in DDPM-declared areas. The contrast is therefore *moratorium with interest waiver* versus *classification and restructuring relief*, not relief versus none. Province-wide shocks, especially Hat Yai's role as a regional hub, are confounded with it.

Two cleaner sources of variation exist inside Songkhla:
- **the per-group credit limits fixed at contract on 2 Dec 2025.** A borrower whose housing limit exceeds THB 3 m, or whose SME limit does, is excluded for that loan type (ว.8158 Q&A 4). The threshold was fixed before BOT announced the package on 15 Dec, which suits a regression discontinuity.
- **radar-flooded versus unflooded sheets.** Both sides were eligible within Songkhla, so this separates the flood from the relief effect.

The nine-province 2025 radar sample mixes the November Level-4 event with the August–September central and north-eastern floods, which had no Level-4 relief. Any Plan B or RQ2 analysis should keep them apart. If DDPM village-level declarations can be obtained, they would make the template-relief timing usable, as intended for 2024. Relief runs 12 months from opt-in, with opt-in by 31 Jan 2026, so relief ends by about early 2027 and a year of post-relief data runs to early 2028.

- (i) `policy_timeline.md` row 25 and identification item 2: Level 4 was designated by Prime Minister's Order 10/2568 under s. 31 of the 2007 Act. The area is Songkhla Province, and the Cabinet resolution sets only the date and the SFI scheme.
- (ii) Add ว.8158 items 2.5–2.6.
- (iii) Add the MoF 164/2568 Cabinet SFI scheme as a separate row.
- (iv) Mark the 2025 Plan B sample as two events.
