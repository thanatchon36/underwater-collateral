# Underwater Collateral: derived data

Derived data behind the working paper and pilot in [https://github.com/thanatchon36/underwater-collateral](https://github.com/thanatchon36/underwater-collateral) (archived at [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23949061](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23949061)):
how flood risk reaches bank capital in Thailand, through official land values, credit under regulatory relief
and a reverse stress test. Everything here is built from **public data only** by the scripts in that repository.
Views and errors are the author's own.

This upload is **tier 1**: the processed and synthetic data. The raw downloads (Treasury land values, Copernicus
Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 scenes, Copernicus DEM, ESA WorldCover, JRC Global Surface Water, UNOSAT, OCHA boundaries,
Bank of Thailand series) are not included; `03_data/README.md` in the repository logs each one's URL, publisher,
access date, licence and SHA-256, and `04_analysis/run_all.py` rebuilds every file here from them.
