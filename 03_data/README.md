Used by `04_analysis/src/rq1_2024_pooled.py`. Sentinel-1 is the one source that maps the 2024 and 2025 seasons nationwide by a single rule; UNOSAT (above) covers only Chiang Rai and Phayao and serves as its validation. Planetary Computer's terrain-corrected product (`sentinel-1-rtc`) needs an account and was not used.

Processed by `04_analysis/src/build_north_covariates.py` to `processed/sheet_north_covariates.csv.gz`: WorldCover shares, mean surface elevation, height above the local valley floor (sheet elevation minus the 5th percentile of sheet elevations in the same 1:50,000 sheet) and distance to permanent surface water (90 m cells with GSW occurrence ≥ 50; includes ponds and reservoirs). The Copernicus DEM is a surface model (buildings and canopy included), so elevations are indicative; FABDEM, which removes them, is a candidate for final estimates.

token saved by `hf auth login`; no credential is in the repository.
